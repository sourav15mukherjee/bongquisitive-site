01 · The Lab

Small lab, sharp instruments.

Bongquisitive is BONGQUISITIVE SOLUTIONS PRIVATE LIMITED — a one-person research and design practice based in Kolkata, India.

Every project here starts as a question about how people think, focus and build — and becomes a tool you can actually use. A game that quietly measures your attention. A forge that turns ideas into agent-ready skill files. An interface you can push around with a cursor.

The lab ships small, measures honestly, and treats play as a serious research method.

03 · Open Investigations

What the lab is asking.

Research in progress — questions first, products as evidence.

  1. 2025 →Active

    Attention as a measurable signal

    Using Split Focus telemetry to model how attention splits across two simultaneous tasks — exploring whether score curves act as cognitive fingerprints over time.

  2. 2025 →Active

    AI-assisted developer workflows

    Studying how generated skill files change day-to-day work with coding agents; feeding the findings back into SkillForge’s generation and scanning heuristics.

  3. 2026 →Active

    Physics-driven interfaces

    Prototyping tactile web interfaces — liquid glass, cloth and fluid simulations — that make information feel manipulable rather than merely readable. You are looking at one right now.

  4. 2026 · seedSeed

    Dual-task learning curves

    Can short daily sessions in attention games measurably improve task-switching? Designing a small longitudinal study around Split Focus training modes.
