Content Policy & Takedown (DMCA)

Effective: September 22, 2025

We respect intellectual property rights. If you believe content hosted by us infringes your rights, send a notice to bongquisitivesolutions@gmail.com with:

  • Your contact details and proof of authority.
  • Exact URL(s) and description of the infringing content.
  • Your good-faith statement that use is unauthorized, and a declaration under penalty of perjury.

Upon valid notice, we may remove or restrict content and notify the user.

Counter-Notice

If you believe removal was mistaken, you may submit a counter-notice with details above. We may restore content per applicable law.
